Optimal transport with electromagnetic cost

Examples in Minkowski spacetime $\ \mathbb{R}^{1+3}$, $\ g = \mathrm{dt}^2 - \mathrm{dx}^2 - \mathrm{dy}^2 - \mathrm{dz}^2$

Constant electric field

$A = -E_0\,\mathrm{x}\,\mathrm{dt}$

$F = E_0\,\mathrm{dt}\wedge\mathrm{dx}$

Coulomb field

$A = \tfrac{q_0}{r}\,\mathrm{dt}$

$F = -\tfrac{q_0}{r^2}\,\mathrm{d}r\wedge\mathrm{dt}$

Constant magnetic field

$A = \tfrac{B_0}{2}(\mathrm{y}\,\mathrm{dx} - \mathrm{x}\,\mathrm{dy})$

$F = -B_0\,\mathrm{dx}\wedge\mathrm{dy}$

Plane wave

$A = a\sin(\omega(\mathrm{t} - \mathrm{x}))\,\mathrm{dy}$

$F = a\omega\cos(\omega(\mathrm{t} - \mathrm{x}))\,(\mathrm{dt} - \mathrm{dx})\wedge\mathrm{dy}$
